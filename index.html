1 SATHI – Seed Traceability Portal, Government of India

SATHI covers seed production, certification, inventory and sale, supporting seed lifecycle and traceability. Seed bag tag/lot number can be used to trace seed details.

https://seedtrace.gov.in/

2 SATHI – Seed Lot Details / Quality Information

Lot/tag information includes germination, physical purity, abnormal seed, diseased seed, insect damage and field inspection details.

https://seedtrace.gov.in/ms014/seedTraceOfTLTag

3 High-Throughput Phenotyping of Seed/Seedling Evaluation Using Digital Image Analysis

Research on digital image analysis for evaluating seed and seedling traits, supporting future image-based seed/crop assessment.

https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/8/5/63

4 Current Optical Sensing Applications in Seeds Vigor Determination

Review of optical and image-based sensing techniques for seed vigor, purity and germination, including rapid and non-destructive approaches.

https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/13/4/1167

5 Non-Destructive Testing of Alfalfa Seed Vigor Based on Multispectral Imaging

Study using multispectral imaging and machine learning to assess seed vigor, viability and germination.

https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/7/2760

6 Advances in Seed Health Testing: Molecular Diagnostics, Imaging, and AI

Review discussing imaging, AI/ML and molecular diagnostics in seed health testing, supporting the proposed AI-based future scope.

https://www.mdpi.com/2674-1024/5/1/15

Why This Matters

AgriTrace integrates seed lot traceability (SATHI) with advanced imaging, AI and non-destructive testing to build a transparent, data-driven seed-to-harvest ecosystem. The references span government infrastructure, high-throughput phenotyping, optical sensing, and machine learning — forming the backbone for next-gen crop performance tracking.
